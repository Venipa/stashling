## Is there a StashDB app for Android?

Yes: Stashling is a free, open-source Android app for StashDB. It browses scenes, performers, studios and tags,
keeps your own bookmarks on the phone, and can send scenes to Whisparr. It is a community project and not affiliated
with StashDB.

## Does it work with other stash-box servers?

Yes. During setup choose **Use a custom server** and enter the server's HTTPS address. Plain HTTP is refused for
stash-box servers.

## How do I sign in to StashDB?

Paste your API key, or sign in once with username and password; Stashling fetches the key and keeps it on the
device. Your password is not stored.

## Does Stashling work with Whisparr?

Yes, with Whisparr v3. Connect it under Settings › Whisparr with its address. You can add and search scenes with
presets and tag rules, pick releases, and follow downloads. A Whisparr on your home network over HTTP or with a
self-signed certificate works too.

## Is Stashling on Google Play?

No. Download the APK from [GitHub Releases](https://github.com/Venipa/stashling/releases/latest). The app checks
GitHub for new versions and installs them after you confirm; pre-releases only if you turn them on in About.

## Which Android version do I need?

Android 12 or newer.

## Does it collect data or show ads?

No. There is no tracking, no analytics and no ads, and no Google Play services. Keys and bookmarks stay on your
phone. Stashling only talks to the StashDB (or stash-box) server and its images, the Whisparr you connect, and GitHub
(update checks and the contributor pictures in About).

## Can I hide images in public?

Yes. Settings › Screen privacy & app lock has **Blur images**, an app lock with biometrics, and a secure screen that blocks
screenshots and blanks the app in recent apps.

## Is it free?

Yes, free and open source under GPL-3.0. If you want to support development, use the **Sponsor** link in About or on
[GitHub Sponsors](https://github.com/sponsors/Venipa).

## Who is it for?

Adults (18+) who already use StashDB or another stash-box server, and people running Whisparr on a home server who
want a phone app for it.
